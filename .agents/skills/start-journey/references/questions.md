# Setup questions

Adapted from Super Brain's MIT-licensed second-brain-setup interview. Ask conversationally, in the owner's language; accept information already supplied and allow questions to be skipped. Do not require a role from a fixed list.

1. Would you prefer a short interview, to paste an assessment (such as CliftonStrengths, DISC or another), or both? For an assessment, extract only practical preferences and context for working together, not the full report. Ask the remaining questions where the assessment does not answer them.
2. What name should I use, what do you do, and what are you working on? What language should I use for our conversation and the vault?
3. Which ongoing Areas of your life or responsibilities do you want in the vault? For each, what do you actually do today and what standards do you want to keep? It is fine to leave these details for later.
4. How should I answer: short or detailed, structured or conversational, and with what tone or degree of challenge? For prose in your vault, what writing style, punctuation or tone should I use or avoid?
5. If your language has grammatical gender, which gender should I use for myself, and which for you?

On a refresh, ask only what changed, not the whole interview. Confirm ambiguous changes before proposing them.
